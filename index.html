WHAT THE SCORE MEANS

Quality is not a price target.

We look at the business itself. The score is one useful input to your research, not a decision made for you.

01

Business, not share price

We measure company quality. We do not score whether a stock is cheap or expensive.

02

Your numbers, your analysis

You enter the figures. There is no automatic data feed behind the spreadsheet.

03

A score, not a signal

Finvlo does not tell you what to buy or sell. You decide what the score means for your research.
