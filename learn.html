01 / COMPANY QUALITY

Quality and price answer different questions.

Quality asks what kind of business a company is. Price asks what the market currently charges for a share. A quality score can’t tell you whether that price is fair.

02 / FUNDS

An ETF is a container, not one business.

An ETF can hold many assets. Its overall profile depends on what it owns and how the fund is structured, so a single-company checklist does not automatically apply.

03 / THE INPUTS

Know where a number came from.

When you enter company figures into a spreadsheet, keep track of the source, reporting period, and units. A neat score cannot fix a mistaken input.
